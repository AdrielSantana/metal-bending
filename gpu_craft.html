Bendcraft 512×512 on WebGPU: the first-person voxel world of the CPU page as it stood on 19 Sep, ported by hand to WGSL with its rays as written (fork!(7n) to 4×4 tiles, a DDA of 60 steps, the 2×2 collapse). W/A/S/D fly, arrows look, I/K up and down, J breaks the block under the crosshair, L places one on its face. The Bend program has since gained walking, jumping and the mouse; this page stays at the render it measured, since the game is written once, in Bend, until Bend targets WebGPU itself. The world is 1024 columns of 32 bits in a 4 KB buffer: an untouched column is computed from the terrain function on the GPU, as the Bend program does at a WNone; an edited one is written by the page, which runs the picking in JavaScript. ?built=300 starts with the benchmark's 300 placed blocks. The CPU page does 20 fps on ten cores. Numbers and the how on the index.
